Transparent Air Curtain


We prepare our transparent door curtain, produced with innovative technology, so that you can easily install it and provide long-lasting use.







Keep Your Air Conditioning Cool Inside in Summer


Minimizes the entry of hot air from outside. Thanks to its magnetic system, it automatically closes after entry and exit.



Keep Cold Air Outside in Winter


Minimizes the entry of cold air from outside. Thanks to its magnetic system, it automatically closes after entry and exit.





Buy once, use for a long time!


With flame-retardant properties and an easy installation system, it can be installed on any door system with just 4 screws. Moreover, you don't need a professional for installation; anyone who can use a drill can easily install it.


With the production and sale of door curtains with magnetic transparent edges and 2 mm thickness; we offer practical and safe solutions that increase the energy efficiency of businesses and living spaces. Our products provide the same performance as electric air curtains with zero energy consumption, creating both an environmentally friendly and economical alternative. Thanks to its specially designed magnetic structure, people experience no difficulty when passing through the door. The magnetic sections open easily, and after passing, the curtain quickly returns to its original form thanks to the weights at the bottom and the thick, durable transparent tarpaulin structure.


The installation process is extremely simple. The transparent curtain can be quickly installed by threading the curtain strips onto the flat hinge system fixed to the top of the door or ceiling with just 4 screws.







Long-Lasting Use


Transparent tarpaulin door curtain systems are very long-lasting thanks to their 2mm net thickness, and provide safe use thanks to their fire resistance certificate. When you're done, you can easily remove and store them thanks to the easy detachable hinge system, and when the weather changes, you can install them again in a short time.













You're always open thanks to the transparent system!

Thanks to its transparent structure, your business door always appears open. The subconscious open image in people's minds is directly proportional to the door's openness, and we provide this.
In door passages, it closes very quickly thanks to its magnetic structure and prevents air passage.

Different from classic transparent strip curtains

Thanks to its magnetic side edges, it provides the same level of temperature difference between inside and outside as a door.

- 2mm thickness provides a flat and taut stance.

About the Product


Transparent Tarpaulin Door Curtain – Magnetic Air Curtain is designed to minimize the entry of cold, hot, dust, and insects from the external environment while protecting the indoor air. It is ideal for markets, warehouses, cold storage rooms, and areas with high traffic.


Thanks to its specially designed magnetic structure, people experience no difficulty when passing through the door. The magnetic sections open easily, and after passing, the curtain quickly returns to its original form thanks to the weights at the bottom and the thick, durable transparent tarpaulin structure. This way, the indoor temperature is maintained, hot-cold air loss is minimized, and dust and air flow are prevented.


The installation process is extremely simple. The transparent curtain can be quickly installed by threading the curtain strips onto the flat hinge system fixed to the top of the door or ceiling with just 4 screws. It does not require professional equipment or special expertise; it becomes ready for use in a short time.
