Transparent Air Curtain


We produce our transparent door curtain with innovative technology, making it simple for you to install and ensuring long-lasting use.







Keep Your AC Cool Air Inside (Summer)


Minimizes hot air from outside entering. Thanks to the magnetic system, it closes automatically after entry and exit.



Keep Cold Air Out (Winter)


Minimizes cold air from outside entering. Thanks to the magnetic system, it closes automatically after entry and exit.






Buy Once, Use for a Long Time!


Flame-retardant and easy to install with only 4 screws on any door system; you do not need a professional, anyone who uses a drill can install it easily.


The installation process is very simple. The clear counterparts are passed through the straight hinge system fixed with only 4 screws to the top of the door or ceiling, allowing quick setup. It does not require professional equipment or special expertise; it becomes ready to use in a short time.








Long-Lasting Use


Transparent tarpaulin door curtain systems are highly durable thanks to their 2 mm net thickness, and the flame-retardant certificate ensures safe use. When you are done, the easy hinge system lets you remove and store it, and when the weather changes you can reinstall it quickly.


With magnetic transparent edges and 2 mm thickness, our door curtains offer practical and safe solutions that improve energy efficiency for businesses and living spaces. Our products deliver the same performance as electric air curtains with zero energy consumption, making them environmentally friendly and economical. Thanks to the specially designed magnetic structure, people pass through the door without difficulty. The magnetic sections open easily, and after passing, the weights at the bottom and the thick, durable clear tarpaulin help the curtain quickly return to its original form.













Stay Open Thanks to the Transparent System!


Its transparent structure makes your workplace door always look open; the sense of openness people perceive is directly proportional to how open the door appears, and we provide that.
During door passages, the magnetic structure closes very quickly and blocks air flow.

Different from Classic Clear Strip Curtains


Thanks to the magnetic side edges, it maintains the interior and exterior temperature difference at the same level as the door.

- 2 mm thickness provides a flat and taut stance.
- B1 fire class ensures safety.
- Acts as an air curtain without consuming energy.







About the Product


The Transparent Tarpaulin Door Curtain – Magnetic Air Curtain is designed to protect indoor air by minimizing the entry of cold, heat, dust, and insects from outside. It is ideal for markets, warehouses, cold rooms, and areas with heavy foot traffic.


Thanks to the specially designed magnetic structure, people pass through the door without any difficulty. The magnetic sections open easily, and after passing, the weights at the bottom and the thick, durable transparent tarpaulin allow the curtain to quickly return to its original form. This preserves indoor temperature, minimizes heat loss, and blocks dust and drafts.


The installation process is extremely simple. The straight hinge system fixed with only 4 screws to the top of the door or ceiling allows you to thread the counterparts of the transparent curtain and complete setup quickly. It requires no professional equipment or special expertise; it becomes ready to use in a short time.

Height Information


  • Bottom weights are shipped already mounted to the product
  • Excess length can be cut with a utility knife
  • Example: for a 194 cm door height, we send the 200 cm product with the bottom weight mounted at 180 cm. After mounting to the door, trim the 6 cm touching the floor with scissors or a utility knife. For proper function, the product should not drag on the floor.

Note: Excess contact with the floor can weaken the magnetic grip.
